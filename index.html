01 · What you will be able to do

Learning outcomes

The course combines HR vocabulary, analytical thinking, performance management, compensation, learning and strategic alignment.

Speak HR

Recognize core HRM elements, terms and practices and communicate them clearly.

Think strategically

Translate company strategy into HRM policies and distinguish practices that do or do not support corporate goals.

Evaluate performance

Understand and evaluate performance-management and appraisal processes.

Design rewards

Identify key elements of compensation systems and understand the logic of pay decisions.

Develop people

Understand learning, development, training and feedback as connected HR processes.

Connect the system

Outline critical success factors in HRM and see links between recruitment, development, performance and reward.

03 · AI contract

Three modes of AI use

Instead of one blanket rule, the syllabus separates tasks by what is actually being assessed.

No AI

Independent mastery

  • In-class exercises
  • Quizzes
  • Exam questions

Purpose: demonstrate your own command of core HR concepts, terminology and calculations.

Limited + disclosed

AI as support

  • Analysis
  • Comparison
  • Case discussion

AI may support brainstorming, drafting or checking, but its contribution must be disclosed and the final judgment must be your own.

AI required

AI as a professional tool

  • Selected capstone cases
  • Initial draft generation
  • Critique + revision + justification

The grade focuses on how well you improve, challenge and justify the AI-assisted output.

06 · Rules & reference

Need to know

Attendance & lateness

Attendance is mandatory. Students may miss up to 20% of classes (no more than 6). Arrival more than 10 minutes late is treated as a missed class.

Academic integrity

Plagiarism, copying, prompting and other forms of unfair work are not allowed. Work that violates the stated AI mode is treated as an academic-integrity issue.

Course material

Garry Dessler, Human Resources Management, 15th Edition, Pearson, 2020.

Teaching format

Interactive lectures by the instructor and student presentations across 30 contact sessions.
